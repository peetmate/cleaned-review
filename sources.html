Feed digestibility: common ranges (printed p. 10.21)

Text on feed digestibility (DE, % of GE). Common ruminant ranges given as about 45-55% for crop by-products and rangelands, 55-75% for good pastures, well-preserved forages and grain-supplemented diets, and 75-85% for feedlot grain diets. Used to justify the 45% DE floor in proposed fix F01. Open the PDF at this page.

Eq. 10.10 (printed p. 27)

Net energy for lactation for sheep and goats when milk yield is unknown: from the lamb's or kid's weight gain from birth to weaning and the milk energy value (EVmilk). Open the PDF at this page.

Eq. 10.14 (printed p. 29)

REM, the ratio of net energy available for maintenance to digestible energy: REM = 1.123 - 4.092e-3 x DE + 1.126e-5 x DE^2 - 25.4 / DE, with DE in %. Open the PDF at this page.

Eq. 10.15 (printed p. 30)

REG, the ratio of net energy available for growth to digestible energy: REG = 1.164 - 5.16e-3 x DE + 1.308e-5 x DE^2 - 37.4 / DE, with DE in %. Open the PDF at this page.

Eq. 10.16 (printed p. 30)

Gross energy: GE = [(NEm + NEa + NEl + NEwork + NEp) / REM + (NEg + NEwool) / REG] / (DE% / 100), MJ/day. The text below it: dry matter intake = GE / feed energy density, default 18.45 MJ per kg DM, and intake is expected at about 2-3% of body weight (over 4% for high-producing dairy cows). Open the PDF at this page.

Review note: The package divides the summed NE terms by the diet's ME density instead (critical issue 1).
